How to use Clip Ink

One finger writes. More fingers edit. One button sends.

The idea in one line: moving the cursor keeps you writing; picking up words lets you act on them. In the app, draw a ? with three fingers to see the short version of this page.

Writing

Do thisTo
Write with one fingerAdd text. Each word appears after a short pause.
TapWrite a dot (or open a drawing you tap on).
Draw ⅃ < >New line · backspace · space.
Draw ^Dictate: speak, and it goes in at the cursor.
Draw a big boxDraw a picture into the note.
Scribble a zigzagDelete the word at the cursor.
Strike a line through wordsDelete those words.

Moving around and selecting

Do thisTo
Hold a word with one fingerSelect it, with handles to adjust and a menu.
Put down two fingers, lift one, dragMove the cursor, like a trackpad.
Drag two fingers togetherScroll the note.
Put down three fingers, lift two, dragSelect from the cursor.
Hold on blank spacePaste.
Tap off a selectionLet it go; the cursor stays at its end.

The selection menu offers spelling fixes, Say (replace the words by speaking), Cut, Copy, Paste and Select all.

Swipe off an edge

FingersLeft · Right · Up · Down
OneMove the cursor a letter or a line. Hold at the edge to keep going.
TwoDelete a word · add a space
ThreeDelete the line · new line · undo · redo

Sending the note

The button at the bottom right shows where your note goes. Tap it to send. It starts on the clipboard every time: tap, and the note is copied and you get a fresh page.

  • Hold the button to choose somewhere else: a text message, email, or any app you’ve added.
  • Or draw that destination’s letter with three fingers: S for a text, E for email, C for the clipboard. The button changes; nothing is sent until you tap it.
  • To add an app or change the letters, open the help (three-finger ?) and tap Gesture mapping.
  • Sending to an app opens it with your note filled in, and keeps your note in Clip Ink in case you change your mind.

Tips

  • The first launch downloads the handwriting model, so do it once with a connection.
  • On the cover screen, open Clip Ink from your cover widgets or launcher. Reopening it from the cover’s recent apps may ask you to open the phone; that’s Samsung’s rule.
  • Pictures you draw can be copied as one image of the whole note, or left out to copy just the text.
