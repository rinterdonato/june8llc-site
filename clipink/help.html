How to use Clip Ink

One finger writes. More fingers edit. One button sends.

New to it? You don’t need the gestures to start: hold the button at the bottom right for Help, Dictate and New drawing. In the app, Help is also a three-finger ? away. The gestures are for when you want to go faster; the buzzes tell you each one was understood.

Writing

Do thisTo
Write with one fingerAdd text. Each word appears after a short pause (you can set how long).
TapWrite a dot, even on a drawing.
Draw ⅃ < >New line · backspace · space.
Draw ^Dictate. The button lights up as a mic that moves with your voice; tap anywhere to stop.
Draw a big box, or hold four fingers stillOpen a new drawing.

Moving around

Do thisTo
Swipe one finger straight off an edgeMove the cursor a letter (← →) or a line (↑ ↓). Hold at the edge to keep going.
Drag two fingers togetherScroll. The cursor stays on its word.
Put down two fingers, lift one, dragMove the cursor like a trackpad. Push against the top or bottom to scroll line by line, faster the further you push.

A buzz tells you two fingers have landed, and another when you lift one into the trackpad.

Selecting

Do thisTo
Hold a wordSelect it, with handles to adjust.
Hold blank spaceSelect the word the cursor is on (or paste, if it isn’t on one).
Put down three fingers, lift two, dragSelect from the cursor, a buzz per word.
Write over a selectionReplace it with what you write.
Tap off it, or put down two fingersLet it go.

The bubble over a selection grows with your text. Its top row offers spelling and grammar fixes and the handwriting’s other readings of a word (handy when it misread you); its icons are Say (replace by speaking), Cut, Copy, Paste and Select all.

Deleting and undoing

Do thisTo
Draw a line through words, or through and backDelete them.
Scribble a zigzag over wordsDelete them; a long buzz as the zigzag forms, the delete when you lift. In blank space, it deletes the word at the cursor.
Scribble a zigzag with three fingersClear the page, after asking. Three fingers up brings it back.

Swipe off an edge

FingersLeft · Right · Up · Down

TwoDelete a letter · add a space. Hold at the edge to keep going.
ThreeDelete the line · new line · undo · redo

Sending the note

The button at the bottom right shows where your note goes. Tap it to send. It starts on the clipboard every time: tap, and the note is copied and you get a fresh page.

  • Hold the button for its list: Help, Dictate and New drawing at the top, then where to send: a text message, email, or any app you’ve added.
  • Or draw a destination’s letter with three fingers: S for a text, E for email, C for the clipboard. The button changes; nothing is sent until you tap it.
  • Sending to an app opens it with your note filled in, then Clip Ink clears the note and closes, ready for the next one. The text is on your clipboard too, in case you change your mind. Email and apps with a subject or title get “Clip Ink” and the date and time there.
  • Hold an empty page to start a note from whatever is on the clipboard.

Drawing

Like a screen-off memo: a black page and your finger. Hold a drawing in your note to open it again. Opening and closing the drawing page gives a double buzz.

One fingerDraw, or erase.
Two fingersPinch to zoom in (up to 10×) for detail; drag to move around.

Four fingers held on an empty pageBack to writing.

✕ · ✓Discard · put the drawing in the note.

Settings

From Help, tap Settings: text size; cursor and scroll speed; how long to wait before writing turns into text; when the page fades while idle; how far drawings zoom; colour pairs for the text and the handwriting; whether to ask before clearing the page; which shortcuts the button’s list shows; the three-finger letters (Gesture mapping); and Report a problem.

Tips

  • The first launch downloads the handwriting model, so do it once with a connection.
  • On the cover screen, open Clip Ink from your cover widgets or launcher. Reopening it from the cover’s recent apps may ask you to open the phone; that’s Samsung’s rule.
  • Whichever way the cover’s screen-size button is set, Clip Ink keeps clear of the cameras, so you always write in the same space.
  • Pictures you draw can be copied as one image of the whole note, or left out to copy just the text.
