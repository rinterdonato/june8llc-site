Clip Ink

Write on your cover screen. No keyboard.

The cover screen of a Galaxy Z Flip is right there when the phone is closed, but typing on it means a cramped keyboard you can’t change. Clip Ink skips the keyboard entirely: write with a finger, anywhere on the screen, and it turns into text a word at a time. When you’re done, one tap puts the note on the clipboard, in a text message, an email, or any app that takes text.

$0.99, one payment · coming soon to Google Play

What it does

Write with a finger

Handwriting becomes text as you go. Recognition runs on the phone itself.

Edit without a keyboard

Move the cursor, select, delete and undo with a few finger gestures, like a trackpad.

Send it anywhere

Clipboard, text message, email, or any app you pick: draw its letter with three fingers to switch.

Dictate and draw

Speak a sentence, or sketch a picture into the note. Spelling mistakes are underlined.

Good to know

  • Made for the cover screen of Samsung’s Galaxy Z Flip, and works on any Android phone running Android 10 or later, on the main screen too.
  • The first time it opens, it downloads Google’s handwriting model (about 20 MB), so it needs a connection once. After that, writing works offline.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Your note stays on your phone until you send it. See the privacy policy.
